# v0.5 ESKF: design and first results (2026-10-20)

Status: design E1–E6 **accepted** (owner); implementation details below are [ASSUMED] tuning.

## Design (accepted)

| | |
|---|---|
| E1 | 16 states: δp, δv, δθ (nav frame, `Ĉ = Exp([δθ]×)C`), δb_a, δb_g (phone frame, lumped slow, random walk: 5e-4 m/s² and 1e-4 rad/s allowed over 180 s), δb_h (barometer offset). Only the bias states get deliberately small Q (DESIGN 5.0). |
| E2 | Mount and lever arm known (baseline); wrong mount is a later mismatch test. |
| E3 | GNSS velocity R inflated for correlation (3.2 × reported σ ≈ 0.32 m/s), position R = (2 × reported σ)², NIS gate χ²(3) 99.9 %. |
| E4 | NHC: body y, z velocity at R = 0, σ 0.1 m/s at 5 Hz; barometer 1 Hz average, σ 0.15 m. |
| E5 | ZUPT from an IMU stop detector (or oracle stops for the upper bound). |
| E6 | 50 seeds (development), along-track error at R, consistency, structural rank check. |

## Implementation choices [ASSUMED]

- Process noise uses effective densities that include vibration: accel 0.02 m/s/√s, gyro
  1e-3 rad/√s (profile VRW/ARW alone would make the filter overconfident).
- Initial attitude by TRIAD: up from the mean specific force, forward from the known mount and
  the GNSS course. Covariance propagated at 10 Hz.
- Stop detector: road-band (2–18 Hz) RMS of |f| < 0.01 m/s², and 1 s std of the < 2 Hz specific
  force < 0.05 m/s², held 0.3 s, filter speed < 2 m/s. The second condition was added after S4
  failed (below): road vibration scales with speed, so band energy alone cannot tell standing
  from creeping, and a launch after a stop was taken as standing. The detector rests on our
  assumed vibration model (DESIGN 5.2 honesty note).

## Defects found while bringing it up

1. Innovation sign: residuals were measurement − prediction while the state is estimate − truth;
   fixed to `z = h(x̂) − y`.
2. Barometer never applied: the first 1 Hz epoch coincided with the start sample and the index
   never advanced. Without the barometer, NHC explained vertical body velocity with nav vertical
   velocity instead of pitch, pitch drifted ~1.5° in 135 s and S1 ended ~0.9 km off. With the
   barometer: pitch held to ~0.1°, S1 ~20 m.
3. Stop detector (above): S4 4.1 km → 54 m.
4. Simulator page: a duplicated key overwrote the ZUPT flag array; canvas width feedback.

## A. Exit re-acquisition (accepted 2026-10-20, owner: robust over fast)

After an outage > 10 s every seed enters a re-acquisition phase [ASSUMED parameters]:
- first 3 s velocity (Doppler) only, no position;
- position sigma inflated ×10 at the start, decaying to ×1 with τ = 8 s;
- the gain is scaled (α ≤ 1) so one update moves the position by at most 2 m; applied to velocity
  updates too, because at the end of a long tunnel P_pv is large and a velocity fix alone moved
  the position by tens of metres; Joseph form keeps P valid for the scaled gain;
- position fixes reporting σ > 10 m are not used (catches the entry degradation; exit multipath
  is not reported by the receiver, so the phase above does that work);
- the phase ends on convergence, not a timer: ≥ 10 s and 5 uncapped position updates in a row
  (a 30 s timer let S4's remaining ~40 m snap back in one step).

A/B, 50 seeds (`reports/0.5.0.dev0/reacq_ab_50.json`); recovered = horizontal error stays below
10 m (≈ 2× open-sky GNSS horizontal rms, 4.2 m):

| | S1 | S2 | S3 | S4 | S5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| largest exit step, p95, off → on | 52 → 4.0 m | 45 → 4.0 | 44 → 5.6 | 76 → 4.2 | 65 → 4.0 |
| median time to 10 m, off → on | 23 → 15 s | 23 → 12 | 24 → 19 | 22 → 23 | 22 → 19 |
| not recovered within 60 s (of 50), off → on | 0 → 0 | 0 → 0 | 1 → 4 | 0 → 16 | 0 → 0 |

The jump disappears everywhere. The price is the one accepted: after the longest outage (S4,
~50 m residual) draining at ≤ 2 m per fix takes longer than 60 s for a third of the seeds. A
residual-proportional cap would trade some of the smoothness back.

## B. Cruise (speed-hold) mode — implemented, NOT accepted (open, default off)

Approved design (2026-10-20): when the filter's own along-track acceleration averaged over 5 s
stays below 0.02 m/s² [ASSUMED], with no GNSS velocity for > 5 s and speed > 3 m/s, hold the
current speed `v_hold` and apply a 1 Hz pseudo-measurement `speed − v_hold = 0` with
`R(Δt) = σ0² + (a_th·Δt)²/3`, σ0 = 0.05 m/s [ASSUMED]. New scenario S6 (as S1, ±2 km/h with
40 s ramps inside the tunnel) is the breaking case.

A/B, 50 seeds, along-track error at the real exit (`reports/0.5.0.dev0/cruise_ab_50.json`):

| | S1 | S2 | S3 | S4 | S5 | S6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rms, off | 20.3 m | 18.3 | 27.6 | 53.5 | 36.2 | 21.9 |
| rms, on (updates independent) | 15.1 | 20.9 | 26.0 | 39.0 | 21.8 | — |
| rms, on (k-th update R×k) | 18.0 | 22.6 | 45.9 | 44.9 | 24.5 | 22.0 |
| mean (e/σ)², off | 0.25 | 0.89 | 0.24 | 0.28 | 0.70 | 0.30 |
| mean (e/σ)², on (R×k) | 11.5 | 5.7 | 33 | 29 | 15 | 25 |
| within 2σ, on (R×k) | 48 % | 54 % | 26 % | 28 % | 36 % | 26 % |

Reading: the accuracy gain is real on S1/S4/S5 but the filter becomes badly overconfident
(σ 4–10 m against 20–45 m realised error), and S3 gets worse. Inflating R by the update count
does not fix it. Root cause: `v_hold` is taken from the filter's own state, so the
pseudo-measurement's error is the state error itself; the update treats it as new, independent
information and removes speed uncertainty that was never observed. Not usable as is; kept in
the code behind `EskfConfig.cruise` (default False) so the numbers can be reproduced.

Candidate fixes (owner to choose):
1. Speed-increment constraint with stochastic cloning: clone the speed each second and apply
   `speed_k − speed_{k−1} = 0` with R = (a_th·1 s)²/3. Consecutive increments are independent,
   the measurement observes only the along-track (accel bias + pitch·g) combination; one extra
   state. Breaks when a sustained acceleration stays below the threshold (S6).
2. Augment the state with an along-track acceleration state (Gauss–Markov, σ ≈ a_th) only in
   cruise mode: the motion model enters as process noise, consistent by construction; larger
   change.
3. Drop B and spend the remaining time on assumption removal (C) and the write-up (D).

### Gradient-state cost: trace check (owner's hypothesis, 2026-10-20)

Owner's mechanism: the barometer's main job here is holding pitch; a constant pitch error δθ gives,
through NHC, a vertical velocity v·δθ and so a height error linear in s, exactly like a pressure
gradient. On a straight constant-grade tunnel their H are identical, so with g_h free the
barometer trend is split between pitch and gradient and the pitch hold loosens; the cost grows
with s (σ_g·s ≈ 9 m of height after 3 km for σ_g = 3e-3, against a 0.15 m barometer).

Trace, S5 seed 0 (`tools/plot_grad_trace.py`, `reports/0.5.1/grad_trace_S5.png`): mean
along-track tilt error in the tunnel −0.018° (off) vs −0.056° (on): the hold is looser, as
predicted. But the 104 m error at the diverge is not a slow leak: it appears as a step at the
braking (1.8 km), where the speed error goes −0.9 m/s in a few seconds and ĝ_h moves −1 m/km at
the same time, then both keep drifting. The deceleration makes pitch briefly observable through the
specific-force coupling, and the strong pitch–gradient cross-covariance built up on the steady
part turns that update into a wrong speed correction. The exit curve later restores the speed
(lateral NHC after the turn). S1 has the same loosening without a manoeuvre to trigger it
(17 → 21 m). Conclusion unchanged: default off; the real fix needs a measured gradient (real
tunnel recording) or a second observable for it.
