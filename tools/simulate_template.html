Results with the current settings

One drive per scenario (seed shown above), so single numbers scatter. "–" in live mode: press the re-run button for these settings; the 50-seed statistics are in REPORT.md §3. Along-track error is estimate − truth (+ ahead). The diverge is where the exit branch leaves the main tunnel; the portal is where the car leaves the tunnel. Three filters run on the same phone data: the 4-state 1D EKF (distance, speed, two biases; the map gives curvature and grade), the 10-state map-constrained filter (adds height, pitch, yaw and gyro terms; no map grade) and the 16-state ESKF. The app shows the selected one (class rule: ESKF on a Note10-class IMU, 10-state on a low-end one), with its σ raised when it disagrees with both others, and a rate-limited follower after the exit. The app's error is along the map (the position the app draws, snapped to the road); the 16-state error is along the true road, so the two differ by the cross-track error's projection (≤ 1 m nominal, ~10 m with the pressure gradient's 74 m sideways). Callout: + late, − early, with the k·σ margin and callout distance from the sliders; red when more than 1 s late. Highlighted columns were re-run live with the LIVE settings; the others are the pre-run drives (map switch applies).
